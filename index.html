What is PenWars

A global storytelling challenge

PenWars brings fans, artists, and writers together to build the next generation of superhuman stories. Compete in creative challenges, work with professional creators, and help shape the Legion of Eccentrics universe.

“Art can help us understand our history, our culture, our lives, and the experience of others — in a way nothing else can.”
Explore the LOE universe

How it works

Three steps to battle

01

Form or join a creative team

Rally your artists and writers — or find a squad inside the community. Every team represents its culture.

02

Receive the challenge & create your character

Each episode drops a creative brief. Design a hero that carries your country's story into the universe.

03

Submit your hero to the LOE universe

The world votes. Winning heroes join the Legion of Eccentrics — canon, forever.
